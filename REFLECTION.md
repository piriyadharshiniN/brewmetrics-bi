# Project Reflection

Working on the BrewMetrics Coffee Sales Dashboard helped me understand how a sales dataset can be transformed into an interactive business intelligence report. I organized the analysis around the sales fact table and the date, city, and product dimensions. Creating relationships between these tables helped me see how a star-schema-style model supports comparisons across locations, products, and time. Dashboard features such as a city slicer and date drill-down let a user explore the data from different perspectives.

The insights from the dashboard made the sales patterns more concrete. Bengaluru is the leading city, with approximately 1.1 million in sales. Chennai ranks second, followed by Hyderabad and Coimbatore. Cold Brew leads product sales at approximately 0.77 million, while Tumbler and Coffee Beans Pack are also among the highest-selling products. The monthly Cold Brew view adds context to that product result: sales increase slightly from April to May 2026, then decline through June and July. Seeing the comparisons and trend together reinforced the value of using multiple visuals to answer different questions, instead of relying on a single overall total.

Creating DAX measures gave me practice expressing business questions as calculations, including total sales, month-over-month growth, a year-to-date running total, item sales ranking, and average sale value. I learned that measures respond to filter context, so a selected city or date range can change the result. Reviewing the measures and their formatting in Power BI is important to make sure that their displayed values are useful and understandable.

GitHub helps track project files and changes, while GitHub Copilot can assist with drafting DAX formulas and documentation. I learned that these tools are most helpful when their output is reviewed against the model and the dashboard. Overall, this project strengthened my skills in data modeling, DAX, report design, and communicating findings. It also reminded me to distinguish observations supported by the dashboard from assumptions that have not been checked.
